Our Mission

Smart City Governance & Management

The Problem We Address

Modern municipalities often face massive operational silos. When a citizen detects a damaged asset, filing reports through telephone queues or obscure email channels leads to long delays, administrative backlogs, and poor resolution tracking.

These delays don't just affect aesthetics; they lead to critical safety hazards, structural degradation, and elevated administrative repair costs.

Connecting People to Public Infrastructure

Core Project Objectives

Our solution bridges the gap between public demand and municipal logistics, transforming how city resources are managed.

  • Empower Citizens: Open, highly usable and direct reporting platforms.
  • Dynamic Scheduling: Immediate triage and work-order assignment algorithms.
  • Granular Telemetry: Sensors tracking city health index scores.
  • Accountability: Clear verification pipelines on the progress of reported tasks.

Frequently Asked Questions
How long does it take for a reported issue to be assigned?
High priority alerts are triaged within 30 minutes, and general issues (like potholes or streetlights) are processed and allocated to specialized sector maintenance teams within 4 to 8 business hours.
Can I report anomalies without choosing precise coordinates?
While pinpointing GPS details on the map ensures rapid dispatcher deployment, you can type addresses or cross-streets into the physical location field, and our triage teams will map it manually.
How do I notify the city about emergency transit bottlenecks?
Submit a transit service alert, flagging high-priority levels. If the blockage represents an immediate danger to lives or assets, please bypass reporting apps and phone civil emergency lines immediately.
Is my privacy guarded when filing community complaints?
Yes, citizen emails and names are stored in secure, encrypted profiles and only shared with the direct dispatch agency. Public issue trackers only list details of the reports, never personal information.
